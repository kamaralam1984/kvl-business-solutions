FREQUENTLY ASKED

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Quick answers to the most common questions about our services.

How fast can you deploy a software or website?
Most ready-made software products go live in 24–48 hours. Custom software takes 4–12 weeks depending on scope. Websites launch in 48 hours to 2 weeks.
Do you provide installation and training?
Yes. Every product includes free installation, configuration, and 2 hours of online team training. Onsite training is available for an additional fee.
What is the pricing structure?
We offer annual subscription or one-time perpetual license for most software. Custom development is project-based. All pricing is transparent — no hidden fees, no surprise bills.
Do you offer cloud and on-premise hosting?
Both. You can run our software on KVL Cloud (managed), your own AWS/Azure/GCP, or on-premise servers. We migrate later at no extra cost.
What about data security and backups?
We are ISO 27001 certified. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Automated daily backups with 30-day retention are included.
How does GPS tracking installation work?
Our technician visits your location, installs the GPS hardware in your vehicle (30 min per vehicle), configures the dashboard and trains your team. PAN-India service.
Do you handle civil and mechanical work too?
Yes — we have dedicated civil engineering and mechanical work divisions. From factory construction to fabrication, we handle end-to-end.
What is your refund policy?
All software comes with a 7-day free trial. If you are not happy in the first 30 days of subscription, we refund 100%, no questions asked.
